# 💫 About Me

🧠 I’m a Computer Science student at Dharamsinh Desai University (DDU), currently focused on Machine Learning, Neural Networks, and Deep Learning.

🚀 Most of my learning comes from building, experimenting, debugging, and understanding how models behave rather than just following tutorials blindly.

🏆 I actively participate in Kaggle competitions and enjoy exploring practical ML workflows through hands-on experimentation.

📚 I prefer learning from books and implementation-based practice because understanding concepts deeply matters more to me than simply making things work.

🏊 Outside tech, I’m also a competitive swimmer and recently secured 3rd place in a city-level swimming competition held in Vadodara.

✨ Swimming taught me consistency, patience, and incremental improvement — the same mindset I bring into coding and machine learning.
